Wallist Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Wallist keeps your tasks on your iPhone. It collects no data, has no accounts, shows no ads and does no tracking.

Your tasks and settings

Your tasks, notes, due dates and settings are stored only on your device, in Wallist's storage. The Wallist shortcut, which runs in Apple's Shortcuts app on your iPhone, reads them to set your Lock Screen and Home Screen wallpaper. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

  • If you choose a photo as your wallpaper background, you pick it with Apple's photo picker. Wallist sees only the photo you choose, not your library, and keeps a copy on your device to draw your wallpaper.
  • If you tap Save Image, Wallist asks for permission to add the wallpaper to your photo library. It can add images but cannot see your other photos.

Tips and Wallist Pro

Tips in the tip jar and Wallist Pro subscriptions are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Wallist never sees your payment details or your Apple ID. To check whether Pro is active, the app asks the App Store on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Feedback

If you choose Send Feedback, your Mail app opens a message to us. We receive only what you decide to send, and use it only to reply and to improve Wallist.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.
  • Wallist makes no network connections of its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting Wallist from your iPhone removes your tasks, settings and saved photo copy. Wallpapers you saved to Photos stay there until you delete them.

Children

Wallist collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
